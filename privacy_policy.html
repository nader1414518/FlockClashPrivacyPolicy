Flock Clash Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Local game data

Game progress, settings, unlocked characters, and currency balances are stored locally on the player's device. Flock Clash does not require an account and does not directly collect a name, email address, contacts, photos, or microphone data.

Advertising

The Android app uses Google AdMob for optional rewarded advertisements. Google and its partners may process device identifiers, approximate network-derived location, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and consent choices. Google User Messaging Platform is used to collect consent where required and to let eligible users revisit their privacy choices.

Player choices

Watching rewarded advertisements is optional. Core gameplay remains available without watching an ad. Local game data can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Online Arena and Wi-Fi rooms

Online Arena matches are relayed by a server operated for the game. To match you with an opponent the app sends your chosen display name, avatar, level, trophy count and fighter deck, plus in-match actions, over an encrypted connection. This information is used only to run the match and is not used for advertising. Local Wi-Fi rooms exchange the same details directly between devices on your local network.

Purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. Flock Clash does not receive your payment details.
